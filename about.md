# Level Recorder

Record your runs as macros and render them to video with **no lag**, completion included. Optional microphone track.

- **Record**: captures only your inputs, so there is no recording overhead.
- **Render**: replays the macro at a fixed timestep and encodes every frame with ffmpeg, so game lag can't drop frames.
- **Mic**: toggle it on before recording; it is muxed into the render.
- **Import**: drop text macros into the `imports` folder (see README).

Open the panel with the Level Recorder button on the pause menu.
